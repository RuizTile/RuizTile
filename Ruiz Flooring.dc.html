About Ruiz Flooring

We install tile in Albuquerque homes and businesses, and have since 2013.

Most of our work is floors, showers and backsplashes for homeowners in the middle of a remodel, plus floors and entries for general contractors and property managers. We handle the whole tile scope: tear-out, substrate prep and waterproofing, layout, setting, grout and clean-up.

Licensed and insured. Estimates are free and in writing. We speak English and Spanish.

Process

1

Walk-through

We look at the space, measure, and talk through layout, material and schedule.

2

Written estimate

Line-item pricing for labor and materials, at no cost. No pressure to book.

3

Prep

Tear-out, leveling, backer board and waterproofing before a single tile goes down.

4

Install

Set, grout, seal, caulk and clean up. We walk the finished job with you.
